Built for the Arc Ecosystem

USYC Integration

ARCDex supports USYC — Arc's yield-bearing stablecoin — as a first-class asset for swaps and liquidity pools, aligning with Arc's stablecoin finance thesis.

Getting Started

Four steps to start

Get testnet tokens and start interacting with ARCDex on Arc Network in minutes.

01

Get Arc Testnet Wallet

Install a compatible wallet and connect to Arc Testnet RPC. Docs have full setup instructions.

02

Claim Testnet Tokens

Use the Arc faucet to get testnet USDC, EURC or USYC. No real funds required.

03

Open ARCDex

Connect your wallet in the app. Your balances will appear automatically once connected.

04

Swap, Pool or Pay

Explore all modules — swap stablecoins, provide liquidity, send a payment, or stake for yield.
